Method

AI agents searched for candidates and wrote independent checkers. The published status of each problem was checked before any search, and a result is reported only after its checks were re-run from scratch. A long computation, or a checker that accepts malformed input, does not count as progress.

What these results mean

The OEIS extensions are exact computations beyond the cached published ranges. BB(6) local transition lemmas leave a global proof obligation open. The HorizonMath searches retained the known constructions. Failed bounded searches do not establish optimality.

Published status is a dated source-search snapshot, with no coverage of unpublished work. See verification commands and limitations.
