Busy Beaver BB(6): partial progress

By Hari Naralasetty (GitHub · LinkedIn) · 2026-10-21

The BB(6) work checked partial results for two candidate machines and left both undecided.

The Busy Beaver problem BB(6) asks for the longest possible running time of a halting six-state, two-symbol Turing machine started on a blank tape.

The checked rules describe individual steps or phases of a computation. They do not show what the machines will do forever.

Technical details

Status and exclusions

The source snapshot starts with 663 published representatives and excludes 92, retaining 571 candidates. Exclusions cover published decisions, explicit blank-orbit equivalences, Cryptids and potential Cryptids, existing research claims and work in progress. Blank spreadsheet status alone is not proof that a machine is open.

The later status refresh retained candidates 24 and 444. Equivalence edges for 444 relate blank-start behavior; they do not decide it. Private and unpublished work is outside this status check.

Certified tools and self-checks

The source tools include a fast simulator, an independent reference simulator, translated-cycler and n-gram CPS certificate checkers, a FAR checker, and a symbolic local-proof kernel. The source self-check compared 500 random machines, reproduced known champion runs, and tested both certificate checkers against 1772 known halters with no false positives. Existing small-machine fixtures and corruptions exercise the checkers; they are not new holdout decisions.

These are custom checker certificates. No new decision has been certified with busycoq or Rocq. Third-party simulator implementations are linked from the source instructions rather than vendored here.

Local lemmas and the proof gap

Candidate 24 has 27 checked local lemmas and candidate 444 has 21, giving 48 total. The fresh source verification rejected 198 corrupted variants. Local transition and word-parser statements do not establish global non-halting.

The missing argument is a closed configuration family reachable from blank tape, preserving the left context through carries, delimiter changes and rebuilding, together with the required word induction. Finite simulations and a counter decrement observed in sampled phases do not close this gap.

Final stage 2c outcome

The six-hour investigation finished after 360 min. Candidate 24 remains undecided. The new local certificates contain 118 rules; verification rejected 470 corrupted variants and checked 3973 independent concrete instantiations. These are additional to the earlier 48 lemmas.

The carry and unary-prefix rules do not supply a closed blank-start configuration family through rebuilding. A proposed guarded family contains hypothetical halting starts, so it cannot establish global safety. Those starts are not asserted blank-reachable. A checked finite blank-start anchor also does not prove non-halting.

The final global blank-start search recorded 8 unknown outcomes. Conditional prefix-boundary searches recorded 10 unknown outcomes and 1 external wall cap; retained-context anchor searches recorded 10 unknown outcomes. No target certificate was accepted. The committed decision registry is empty. A decision may be reported only after the full source make verify-decisions accepts a registered certificate.

Reproduce the distributed checks

Run make -C bb6 verify to check the local proofs, reject corrupted variants, replay concrete instantiations and confirm the distributed registry is empty. This portable target does not rerun the entire source simulator campaign.

Read the local checker, proof objects and source report. The bbchallenge holdout lists explain the upstream lists.
