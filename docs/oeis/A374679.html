New terms for OEIS A374679: compositions with strictly increasing anti-run leaders

By Hari Naralasetty (GitHub · LinkedIn) · 2026-10-21

This page gives 487 independently checked new terms for OEIS A374679: compositions with strictly increasing anti-run leaders.

Definition

Number of integer compositions of n whose leaders of anti-runs are strictly increasing.

Quoted from OEIS A374679, as cached in the source workspace.

Published range and extension

The cached published range was 0 through 25. The extension adds indices 26 through 512, giving 487 new terms. These terms have not yet been submitted to the OEIS.

Download the complete A374679 b-file, published and new rows.

Two computation methods

Dynamic programming on weight, previous part and last anti-run leader.

Truncated generating functions for complete anti-runs.

An anti-run is split at equal adjacent parts; successive leaders must strictly increase.
