New terms for OEIS A391646: least integers with a specified sum of prime-factor remainders

By Hari Naralasetty (GitHub · LinkedIn) · 2026-10-21

This page gives 24 independently checked new terms for OEIS A391646: least integers with a specified sum of prime-factor remainders.

Definition

a(n) is the least integer k such that the sum of the prime divisors of k, each taken modulo its exponent in the prime factorization of k, equals n.

Quoted from OEIS A391646, as cached in the source workspace.

Published range and extension

The cached published range was 0 through 13. The extension adds indices 14 through 37, giving 24 new terms. These terms have not yet been submitted to the OEIS.

Download the complete A391646 b-file, published and new rows.

Two computation methods

Multiple-choice multiplicative knapsack over prime powers in Python.

C++ branch-and-bound over distinct prime factors, with optimistic suffix bounds.

Prime factors of exponent one contribute zero and can be removed. Both searches use a finite cap; the computed minima must lie inside it.

Reproduce

From the repository root, run make -C oeis verify. It recomputes all extensions with both methods and compares every row, including the published overlap. C++ and Python are required.
